Presenting with Cueline

  1. Load your script and set a target length. Cueline works out the delivery rate that lands it, and reports your drift against it while you speak.
  2. Float the prompter and position it directly beneath your camera. Keep the column narrow; the less your eyes travel, the more you appear to be speaking rather than reading.
  3. In Zoom, share a window or a single browser tab. Not the entire screen.
  4. Roll. Either at a set rate, or with voice follow, which tracks what you actually say.

Screen sharing

A web page cannot exclude itself from screen capture; only a native application can. Sharing a window or a single tab sends only that surface, so the floating prompter is never in it. Sharing the entire screen sends everything on it, including the prompter. Cueline asks you to confirm this once, the first time you float the window.

Script format

# Headinga section you can jump between
> Notea cue to yourself — dimmed, never counted
---a rule, for a slide change
==stress==a word to hit — drawn per the Stress setting
**bold** *italic*emphasis
- itema list item

Headings and cues are excluded from the word count, so the timing reflects only what you will say aloud.

Before you share your screen

In Zoom, share a window or a single browser tab. Do not share the entire screen — a web page cannot hide itself from screen capture, so the floating prompter would be visible to everyone in the call.
