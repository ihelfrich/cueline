Presenting on Zoom with Cueline

  1. Write or paste your script, then set a target length. Cueline works out the words-per-minute that lands it and warns you when you drift.
  2. Press “Float over Zoom”. The prompter moves into a small always-on-top window. Drag it right under your webcam so your eyes stay near the lens.
  3. In Zoom, share a window or a Chrome tab — not “Entire Screen”. A window share only contains the app you picked, so the floating prompter is never in it.
  4. Start it rolling. Turn on voice follow and it tracks what you actually say; otherwise it scrolls at your chosen speed and you nudge it with the buttons.

The one thing to get right

If you share Entire Screen, your audience sees everything on it, including the floating prompter. Browsers are not allowed to hide a window from screen capture — only a native app can do that. Share a window or a tab and you are fine. Cueline shows a reminder in the bar at all times.
